Critical Advisory

On-prem SharePoint RCE in CISA KEV — tied to Warlock ransomware

CISA added CVE-2026-45659, a remote-code-execution flaw in on-prem Microsoft SharePoint Server (CVSS 8.8, untrusted-data deserialization), to its KEV catalog on 1 July after confirming active exploitation, with a federal remediation deadline of 4 July. An authenticated user with only Site Member rights can run code; Microsoft rated it “less likely” when patching in May, and Storm-2603 (Warlock ransomware) has exploited on-prem SharePoint since 2025.

How to read the indicators. Exploitation and CISA KEV tags reflect status at generation time — “No known exploitation / Not in CISA KEV” means none was reported as of this edition, not that risk is absent. Suggested SLAs are starting points (Critical ≈ 7 days, High ≈ 30 days) to adapt to your own risk tolerance. Owner and status notes you enter are saved only in your local browser and are not transmitted anywhere.

Methodology. Compiled on-demand by searching authoritative security sources including CISA advisories and the KEV catalog, and reporting from BleepingComputer, the SANS Internet Storm Center, Dark Reading, The Register, The Hacker News and SecurityWeek, plus vendor and product advisories (Microsoft, Oracle, Cisco, Citrix, Progress, SimpleHelp, Fortinet) and Sysdig threat research. Default lookback: 7–30 days. Severity tiers are editorial judgments based on described impact; consult the linked source for official CVSS scores and affected versions. “Claimed” / “reported” items are unverified assertions; “confirmed” items have vendor or agency acknowledgment.

This dashboard is a navigator, not a replacement for direct subscriptions. For time-critical advisories, subscribe to CISA and your vendors' PSIRT feeds directly.
