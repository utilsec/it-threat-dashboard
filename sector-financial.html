On 9 October 2026 the FBI announced the arrest in Pennsylvania of a Canadian national it links to the ShinyHunters extortion group; The Hacker News, citing reports, says he was involved in the hack of the FBI’s own jobs portal. On 6 October FBI Cyber Division Assistant Director Brett Leatherman said the jobs-portal breach happened because a contractor failed to apply a security patch issued specifically for the platform; Reuters’ sources say the platform was Oracle PeopleSoft and the firm Accenture, and the FBI removed the contractor. Mandiant assesses that ShinyHunters is exploiting a bypass of CVE-2026-35273, a missing-authentication flaw in PeopleSoft PeopleTools 8.61 and 8.62 rated CVSS 9.8 and in CISA’s KEV catalog since 12 June, by URL-encoding requests so they evade a WAF rule that blocks the PeopleSoft Environment Management Hub endpoint; the bypass has no CVE of its own. Update 28 October: the Zero Day Initiative says the issue behind CVE-2026-35273 is three chained flaws in two PeopleSoft components — a server-side request forgery in the Integration Gateway lets an attacker reach the Environment Management Hub, which trusts requests from the server itself, and run code without logging in.
