GTIG links the campaign to the group’s use of BeyondTrust Remote Support CVE-2026-102764 (covered in Threat Intelligence). No victim organisation has been named.

Why it matters. vCenter manages the virtual servers most agencies run on, and an attacker who controls its single sign-on can mint administrator access to the whole estate. A state actor exploiting it for two months before disclosure means patching alone does not answer whether you were already compromised (editorial assessment).

Why it leads. It is an actively exploited flaw in a product nearly every agency runs, with a confirmed state-backed actor and a three-day federal deadline.
