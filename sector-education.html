Top Story

DC Public Schools discloses breach of a summer-registration app exposing student data

DC Public Schools said an unauthorized third party accessed a web-based Summer Learning registration application, potentially exposing student and guardian details such as names, IDs, dates of birth, schools, grade levels and contact information. The district removed the affected data, notified law enforcement and reported no evidence of misuse, adding that it does not collect Social Security numbers or family financial data. Even a single exposed third-party app can put a large student population's records at risk.

Critical Advisory

N-able N-central authentication bypass in CISA KEV — RMM admin takeover exploited

CISA added CVE-2026-18577 (CVSS 8.2), an authentication-bypass flaw in N-able N-central, to its KEV catalog on 3 August after confirming exploitation. The bug lets a remote, unauthenticated attacker gain administrative control of an N-central server; observed intrusions abused the platform's Take Control feature to reach managed endpoints and set up tunneling for persistence. N-central is a widely used RMM platform and many school districts rely on MSPs that run it, so a compromised server can cascade to every managed endpoint. Fixed in 2026.3 HF1.

High Advisory

Cisco Secure FMC static-credential flaw exploited as a zero-day

Cisco warned that CVE-2026-20316, a static-credential flaw in Secure Firewall Management Center (FMC), has been exploited as a zero-day. Hardcoded credentials for a low-privilege account let an unauthenticated remote attacker log in and read sensitive data; Cisco rated it High despite a 5.3 base score because the access can be chained with other FMC flaws to escalate. Hot fixes are available for affected 7.0–10.0 releases, and Cisco simultaneously patched a critical FMC RCE (CVE-2026-20079, CVSS 10.0). FMC manages perimeter firewalls at many universities.

High Advisory

Microsoft July Patch Tuesday: exploited AD FS and SharePoint zero-days

Microsoft's July 2026 Patch Tuesday fixed 622 CVEs, including two zero-days already exploited in the wild and since added to CISA's KEV: CVE-2026-56155 (CVSS 7.8), a privilege-escalation flaw in Active Directory Federation Services, and CVE-2026-56164 (CVSS 5.3), a missing-authentication flaw in on-prem SharePoint Server (2016/2019/Subscription Edition) that a remote attacker can abuse without user interaction. AD FS underpins single sign-on and SharePoint is common on campus intranets, so both warrant priority patching in education IT.
