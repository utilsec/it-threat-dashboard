Optimizely supply-chain attack: injected JavaScript reached 178 organisations’ websites, including American Eagle, carrying card skimmers, crypto-address swappers and cookie theft — days before Black Friday

Optimizely has confirmed that attackers abused a flaw in the signup flow of Optimizely Experimentation, its A/B-testing product, to inject JavaScript into customer projects. Any retailer that loads experimentation, analytics or tag-manager scripts on checkout or login pages carries the same exposure.
