Healthcare participants cited differing definitions across CISA, SEC and HIPAA reporting rules, said HIPAA’s security and privacy restrictions appear to conflict with HHS’s prohibition on unreasonably limiting access to health data, and warned that confusion could delay reporting by small providers with limited compliance staff. Participants suggested streamlining definitions and thresholds, consolidating oversight under one agency such as CISA, and closer collaboration with industry; these are their suggestions, not GAO recommendations. For incident-response planning, the practical step is a single matrix of which regulator gets which report, by when and under which definition.
