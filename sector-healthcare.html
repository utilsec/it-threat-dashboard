Ransomware at revenue-cycle vendor Unlimited Technology Systems exposes 442,000 patients

Practice-management and revenue-cycle vendor Unlimited Technology Systems began notifying at least 442,000 patients across multiple states that an October 2025 ransomware attack encrypted systems in its commercial datacenter and accessed their data. Research links hospital ransomware to higher patient mortality as Black Hat and HIMSS launch a joint healthcare summit

Coverage tied to the first joint Black Hat × HIMSS healthcare summit (4 August 2026, Las Vegas) highlighted peer-reviewed research finding that in-hospital mortality for patients already admitted during a ransomware attack rises materially, drawn from Medicare claims linked to confirmed incidents rather than modeling. When ransomware disables systems, clinicians lose medication histories, allergy records and imaging; the disruption also spills to neighboring hospitals that see surges in cardiac and stroke cases. The takeaway: healthcare cyber risk is a patient-safety issue, not just a data-loss one.

Critical Advisory

N-able N-central authentication-bypass exploited in the wild — added to CISA KEV

CISA added CVE-2026-18577, an authentication-bypass flaw (rated CVSS 8.2) in N-able N-central, to its KEV catalog on 3 August after confirmed customer compromises, setting a 6 August federal remediation deadline. N-central is a remote monitoring & management platform many MSPs use to run downstream client environments — including clinics and hospitals — so a full server takeover lets attackers abuse the built-in Take Control feature to pivot into managed endpoints and deploy persistence. It follows an incomplete fix for an earlier flaw; N-able ships the fix in 2026.3 HF1.

High Advisory

Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center static-credential zero-day exploited in the wild

Cisco warned that CVE-2026-20316 (CVSS 5.3), a static/hard-coded credential for a low-privilege account built into Secure Firewall Management Center (FMC), is being exploited in zero-day attacks. An unauthenticated remote attacker can use the credentials to reach sensitive data and, chained with other issues, escalate privileges on the appliance that governs an organization’s firewall policy — a high-value target in hospital and payer networks. Cisco has released hot fixes for affected 7.0–10.0 versions, and CISA has added it to KEV.

Microsoft’s July Patch Tuesday fixes two exploited zero-days in AD FS and SharePoint

Microsoft’s July 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed a record volume of flaws and, most urgently for defenders, two actively-exploited zero-days added to CISA KEV: CVE-2026-56155, an elevation-of-privilege bug in Active Directory Federation Services (CVSS 7.8), and CVE-2026-56164, a missing-authentication flaw in SharePoint Server 2016/2019/Subscription Edition (CVSS 5.3) that an unauthenticated attacker can exploit remotely to gain privileges. Policy Regulation

HHS moves the HIPAA Security Rule overhaul to long-term actions — final rule not expected until 2027

HHS has shifted the proposed HIPAA Security Rule amendments (RIN 0945-AA22) to its long-term regulatory agenda, with July 2027 now the anticipated timeframe for final action. The January 2025 proposal would make today’s “addressable” safeguards mandatory — multi-factor authentication, encryption, asset inventories and network maps, stronger risk analysis and business-associate oversight, and tighter incident-response and recovery requirements. The delay does not change current obligations: the Office for Civil Rights keeps enforcing the existing Security Rule, and MFA and encryption remain expected. No new deadline yet — planning item.
