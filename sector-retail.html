Top Story

Magecart skimmers hide command-and-control in Ethereum smart contracts to evade takedowns

Source Defense researchers detailed a web-skimming campaign that stores its routing infrastructure inside Ethereum smart contracts instead of hard-coding attacker domains in the checkout skimmer. Hosting command-and-control data on an immutable blockchain lets operators rotate infrastructure on demand and blunts traditional domain takedowns. The skimmer clones legitimate payment fields on e-commerce checkout pages and harvests card data, personal details and device information. Retailers should enforce script integrity (SRI/CSP), inventory and monitor third-party scripts on payment pages, and watch for unauthorized checkout changes.

Chick-fil-A warns customers after credential stuffing hits loyalty accounts

Chick-fil-A began notifying customers on 20 July that attackers used credentials stolen in unrelated third-party breaches to log into loyalty accounts on its website and app during a June credential-stuffing attack. Exposed data may include names, emails, loyalty/membership and mobile-pay numbers, QR codes, the last four digits of payment cards, account credit balances and optional profile details. The chain reported affected residents to multiple state attorneys general.

Critical Advisory

N-able N-central authentication bypass added to CISA KEV after MSP compromises

CISA added CVE-2026-18577 (CVSS 8.2), an authentication-bypass-and-account-takeover flaw in N-able N-central, to its KEV catalog on 3 August after confirmed in-the-wild exploitation, with a 6 August federal remediation deadline. Attackers gain admin access to N-central servers and abuse the built-in Take Control feature to pivot into managed endpoints. Because many retailers rely on managed service providers running N-central for remote monitoring, one compromised server can expose multiple downstream retail environments. Fixed in 2026.3 HF1.

Microsoft July Patch Tuesday fixes AD FS and SharePoint zero-days under active attack

Cisco Secure FMC static-credential flaw exploited as a zero-day

Cisco disclosed CVE-2026-20316, a static/hard-coded-credential flaw in Secure Firewall Management Center (releases 7.0 through 10.0) that lets an unauthenticated remote attacker log in to a low-privilege account and access sensitive data. Adobe Commerce & Magento patch 15 flaws, including a 9.6 file-upload bug

Adobe's APSB26-73 bulletin fixes 15 vulnerabilities in Adobe Commerce and Magento Open Source, led by CVE-2026-48356 (CVSS 9.6, unrestricted file upload) alongside authorization-bypass, stored-XSS and input-validation issues. PCI DSS 4.0 client-side payment-page requirements in force for card-handling retailers

The future-dated PCI DSS 4.0 requirements — including stronger anti-skimming controls for payment pages (script inventory and integrity monitoring under requirements 6.4.3 and 11.6.1) — are now mandatory, directly relevant to Magecart-style attacks. Retailers processing cards should confirm their assessors' scope covers client-side payment-page protections.
