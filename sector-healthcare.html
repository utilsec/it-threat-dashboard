AdaptHealth confirms 4.1 million people exposed after a contractor’s cloud account was talked open

AdaptHealth, one of the largest home medical equipment and supplies providers in the United States, has confirmed through SEC and HHS filings that 4,115,802 individuals had data exposed in a breach its filings date to 5 June 2026. The contractor detail is the part most health systems under-model — the privileged account did not belong to AdaptHealth staff, and vendor and contractor identities frequently sit outside the conditional-access, session-length and privileged-access rules an organisation applies to its own workforce.
